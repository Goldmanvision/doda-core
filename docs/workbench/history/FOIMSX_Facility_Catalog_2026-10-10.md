# FOIMS-X Facility Catalog - 2026-10-10

Added Overview/Facilities navigation and a read-only facility catalog screen.
All facility rows, including drafts, are browsable through dropdown and
Previous/Next navigation. The left CRT shows the selected facility, linked
capabilities, global staffing baselines, facility-specific overrides and
qualification references. Details are paginated.

Catalog flags describe authoring status, not live construction or staffing.
Baselines and overrides are shown separately; no effective staffing values
are inferred. Refresh reloads the catalog without resetting sandbox state.
No purchases, construction, assignments, CSV writes or native/game changes.
