# Single-vehicle capacity preview v111

Read-only arithmetic preview on Equipment / Vehicle Types.
Uses a snapshot of the current form capacity; does not Apply, Save or Export.
Compares a positive whole-number hypothetical team count with one vehicle.
Reports fits/exceeds, unused capacity and members over capacity.
No multiple-vehicle count, pooling, driver-seat policy or allocation inferred.
Task staffing, prerequisites, active status and inventory are not checked.
A capacity fit does not imply a task can run.
Vehicle catalog, source capacities and export schemas remain unchanged.
Expected tests: 624 baseline; 636 after installation.
Manual preview check required after successful installation.
